# MF1S50 — MIFARE Classic EV1 1K Tag Datasheet — Key Sections

Document: `~/Books/Hardware-and-Systems/Components/MF1S50.pdf`
(NXP, "MF1S50yyX/V1 MIFARE Classic EV1 1K", Rev. 3.2, 36 pages)

The chip inside the cards and key fobs (the tag), not the reader. It is the tag-side
authority for what the reader sends and what comes back. The reader chip (RC522) is in
[mfrc522.md](mfrc522.md). Page numbers below are the "N / 36" printed on each page.

| Topic | Section | Page |
|-------|---------|------|

| Transaction flow and typical times (~2.5 ms anticollision without collision) | Figure 4 | 6 |
| Data integrity: parity bit per byte, CRC, bit count checks | 8.3 | 6-7 |
| RF interface: ISO/IEC 14443A, odd parity per byte, LSB first, max frame 163 bits | 8.5 | 7 |
| Memory layout, manufacturer block (UID is in block 0) | 8.6 | 7-8 |

| Frame timing (tag answers at least 87 us after the reader's frame) | 9.2 | 14 |
| ACK / NAK codes | 9.3 | 15 |
| **ATQA** (`00 44h` 7-byte UID, `00 04h` 4-byte UID) and **SAK** (`08h`) | 9.4, Tables 11-12 | 15 |

Not in this document: the check byte (BCC) in the anticollision reply, the meaning of
the second byte in `93h 20h`, and the cascade tag value. They belong to ISO/IEC 14443-3
(the document's Ref. 4), which is not in `Components/`. Mark anything taken from there
as unverified.
