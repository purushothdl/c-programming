03 · three traps the datasheet sets

Why the order matters

The flags start dirtyAt reset the flag register already reads 0x14: two flags are on. Clear them before each exchange, or an old flag looks like a new event (p.39).
The receiver starts offCommandReg is 0x20 at reset and bit 5 means "receiver off". Writing a plain 0x0C (Transceive) clears it. Writing 0x2C would leave it off (p.38).
Transceive never stops itselfAfter the reply, or after a timeout, we must write Idle (0x00). The antenna must also be on first, or nothing is sent (p.48, p.72).
04 · the check byte

What a 4-input XOR means

For each bit column, count the ones. An odd count gives 1, an even count gives 0. Click a bit in the received UID to damage it.
